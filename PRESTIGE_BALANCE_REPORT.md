# Prestige model diagnostic

Production economy model, fixed seed 431, 90-minute cap, starter/seed-only purchase policy. No automatic reset is performed. Uses real completed-round cycle earnings, the authored $100M threshold and exponent 0.45. Each required strike must connect; failed chains stop. Six-second menus, modeled strike delays, three varieties and bounded production are inherited assumptions. This is not human playtesting.

| Accuracy | Completed-round earnings by 90m | First eligible prestige |
|---|---:|---|
| 55% | $14.6M | Not reached |
| 80% | $127.0M | 74.0 min |
| 100% | $251.5M | 45.5 min |

A staged legitimate first claim and one-seed Permanent Slice Value purchase changes the production RoundSession's first ordinary cut from $1 to $1.25. This benefit is immediate and survives later prestiges. Starting Cash reveals afterward, costs two seeds initially and grants $5 per level only after a future reset; it never adds qualifying earnings.

The $100M threshold was chosen provisionally near the existing 80%-accuracy model's 75-minute earnings. Accuracy/purchase-policy spread is substantial; do not claim 60–90-minute human balance or change older prices from this diagnostic alone. Equipment, spatial fragment accessibility, human purchase choices, missing later content, prestige upgrades/events and later reset timing are not modeled.
