Each fixture earns 200 seeds through qualifying income and prestige, pays Permanent Slice Value 1, Starting Cash 1, Jackpot Harvest and Jackpot Multiplier 1, then the stated Ultimate levels. Normal funding and prerequisite/equipment/preparation orders are real. Fixture income is excluded. Critical roll inputs are prescribed as no proc, earliest available proc, or second-strike proc; locked abilities remain locked through production resolve. These are conditional accepted-hit sequences, not measured proc rates, player accuracy, blade geometry, flight/fragment timing, event frequency, round income rate, seed ROI or FPS.

Ultimate costs 5/10/20 seeds and requires its own Jackpot gate plus Jackpot Multiplier 1. Paid potential is ×1.5/×2/×2.5, captured at spawn. Only a whole-root split on accepted strike one receives that multiplier; any earlier resistance hit spoils it. Misses/stationary passes are excluded before accepted strikes and are verified by scene/input tests. Ordinary price/combo/wealth apply once after the conditional root value. Critical damage may cross the first-hit threshold but never doubles money directly; later criticals cannot repair spoiled eligibility. Every recut uses ordinary captured value, independent of the root bonus. Shared family limits, unique event counts and event scheduling remain unchanged. Rows establish conditional accounting, not event-income forecasts, human balance or ideal seed choices. Costs remain provisional; earlier reports retain their scopes.
