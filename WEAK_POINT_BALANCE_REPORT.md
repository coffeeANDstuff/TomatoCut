Ordinary profiles use explicit funding and actual purchases. Aimed damage applies only on the assumed fraction of accepted root strikes; this is a prescribed diagnostic input, not an in-game random weak-point roll or measured player aim. No geometry, surface visibility, input sampling, event scheduling, round timer, earnings or FPS is simulated.

Giant Weak Points costs 3/6/12 seeds for ×1.5/×2/×2.5 aimed whole-root damage. Fresh starter hits need six ordinary strikes, four all-aimed level-one strikes and three all-aimed strikes at levels two/three. Threshold saturation can make a later level save no additional strike in a particular profile. Critical damage multiplies composed knife/normal/permanent/preparation strength before the weak multiplier, once each, with no extra ability roll for aiming. Cut rewards are constant within each cultivation profile; the prepared profile's actual Roma/Better Prices investments explain its different captured payout. Fragments retain two resistance and no aiming bonus.

This is a damage threshold/assumption diagnostic, not human aim conversion, expected event income, prestige ROI or late-game performance. Actual swept blade geometry, transformed surface visibility, tiny/back-facing rejection, same-stroke protection, root/recut/captured-value accounting, one marker, bounded pools, settings, durable saves and resets are verified separately by scene/input/pack tests. Seed costs, damage coefficients and target readability remain provisional pending human play. Earlier reports retain their original scopes.
