# Tomato Cut v0.5.0 — Botanical discovery

**[Download the FULL PACKAGE](https://github.com/coffeeANDstuff/TomatoCut/releases/download/v0.5.0/TomatoCut-Full-v0.5.0.zip)** — Windows game, complete editable Godot source, Blender art, tests and handoff documentation in one ZIP.

Unzip it and run **Windows/TomatoCut.exe**. If Vulkan is unavailable, use **Windows/Play-Compatibility.bat**. No editor or installer is required to play. Open **Source/project.godot** in Godot 4.6.3 standard to continue development.

- New discoveries place quiet red seed indicators on the seed-catalog and relevant category icons.
- Only entries actually exposed in the scroll area are marked read; hidden and off-screen entries retain their indicators.
- Newly exposed illustrations/labels fade in as a botanical vine grows. Reduced Motion skips the animation.
- Read/unread state saves immediately, survives revisits, and migrates existing v1/v2 saves without flagging every old entry as new.
- Existing Standard Red, Cherry, Roma, durable slicing, recuts, cultivation, automatic harvest and saves are preserved.
- Downloadable Full, Windows-only and Source archives now accompany the version tag, with SHA-256 manifests and build/source identity.

This is a development milestone: three of 25 normal varieties, one of 25 knives and seventeen upgrades are integrated. Special Cutting Abilities, equipment, prestige/events and remaining content are still in development. Native Windows execution, Forward+ GPU rendering, audible review and target-PC 60 FPS remain unverified. See Source/PROGRESS.md, Source/ROADMAP.md and Source/TESTING.md for tested behavior and the exact next task.
