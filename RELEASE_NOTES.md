# Tomato Cut v0.10.0 — Golden Harvest

**[Download the FULL PACKAGE](https://github.com/coffeeANDstuff/TomatoCut/releases/download/v0.10.0/TomatoCut-Full-v0.10.0.zip)** — Windows game, complete editable Godot project, original Blender/GLB/SVG/WAV art, tests and handoff documentation.

Unzip and run **Windows/TomatoCut.exe**. **Windows/Play-Compatibility.bat** supplies OpenGL fallback. No editor/installer is required to play; open **Source/project.godot** with Godot 4.6.3 standard to continue development.

- The archive’s **Special Events** category reveals after Starting Cash 1. Purchase **Golden Harvest for 1 Tomato Seed** to unlock rare automatic Golden Tomatoes. Normal cultivation cannot unlock them; other events remain separately unavailable.
- **Golden Frequency** adds four percentage points per level (8% base → 20% maximum). **Golden Value** raises the family multiplier from **20× to 50× the best eligible normal cut value**. Each improvement costs 2 seeds initially, doubles its price per level and has three levels. Existing price/combo/permanent bonuses apply afterward once.
- Automatic checks start at four seconds and repeat every four seconds, with twelve-second cooldown, one active family, two events per round and shared spawn/fragment budgets. Skipped checks do not accumulate bursts. There are no manual spawn controls.
- Original seven-lobed golden fruit has its own crown/curved stem, seeded amber interior, prepared halves/wedges/eighths, engraved archive plate, quiet cue and golden juice. Recuts use the usual shared cut budget and one unique-fruit count. The normal Sungold variety remains separate future content.
- Results show round/lifetime Golden Tomatoes. Real paid event investments, discovery and lifetime counts survive reload and prestige. Confirmed New Game clears them while retaining preferences. Earlier schema-5 saves load with zero golden count and no free event unlocks; v1–v4 migrations remain supported.
- **545 headless**, **147 rendered GUI**, and **60 embedded-pack checks per target** pass. Both exports, Linux headless boot, fresh source import and full-package integrity are delivery gates. See Source/TESTING.md for evidence and limits.
