# Tomato Cut v0.6.0 — Critical Slice

**[Download the FULL PACKAGE](https://github.com/coffeeANDstuff/TomatoCut/releases/download/v0.6.0/TomatoCut-Full-v0.6.0.zip)** — Windows game, complete editable Godot source, Blender art, tests and handoff documentation.

Unzip it and run **Windows/TomatoCut.exe**. Use **Windows/Play-Compatibility.bat** for the OpenGL fallback. No editor or installer is required to play; open **Source/project.godot** in Godot 4.6.3 standard to continue development.

- Critical Slice activates automatically on accepted whole-fruit and fragment strikes. Its first level costs $25 after Roma Unlock + Extra Cuts 1; five levels grant 8%–40% chance of double cutting damage.
- A critical can split a fresh Roma in one stroke at Sharp Edge 1. Completed cuts keep their usual payouts and shared family limits.
- The seventh Abilities category now contains its real purchase branch, original icon, live current/next chance and discovery feedback.
- Harvest Dominance and catalog forecasts account for expected critical damage while keeping the normal three-strike safety gate.
- A short local label and original impact cue respect existing feedback preferences and reduced motion. No extra input or camera shake is added.
- Save compatibility, ordinary slicing/recuts, three-variety automatic harvest and botanical discovery are preserved. The economy report includes explicit critical damage diagnostics and balance limitations.
- The release workflow uses the Node.js 24 checkout runtime and publishes verified Full/Windows/Source archives plus checksums.

This is a development milestone: three of 25 normal varieties, one of 25 knives and eighteen upgrades are integrated. The seven-category catalog foundation is complete; equipment is next. Remaining content, prestige/events, production polish and human balance remain on the roadmap. Native Windows execution, Forward+ GPU rendering, audible review and target-PC 60 FPS remain unverified. See Source/PROGRESS.md, Source/ROADMAP.md and Source/TESTING.md for evidence and the exact next task.
