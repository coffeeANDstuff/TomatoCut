# Tomato Cut v0.9.0 — The rare-seed archive

**[Download the FULL PACKAGE](https://github.com/coffeeANDstuff/TomatoCut/releases/download/v0.9.0/TomatoCut-Full-v0.9.0.zip)** — Windows game, complete editable Godot project, original art, tests and handoff documentation.

Unzip and run **Windows/TomatoCut.exe**. Use **Windows/Play-Compatibility.bat** for OpenGL fallback. No editor/installer is required to play; open **Source/project.godot** with Godot 4.6.3 standard to continue development.

- The envelope icon opens an original illustrated **Rare-seed Archive** from Home, Results or menus. Its live ledger shows completed-round qualifying earnings and a diminishing-return Tomato Seed quote. Spending money does not reduce qualifying earnings; unfinished rounds and starting cash do not add them.
- First-seed threshold is provisionally **$100M**, with exponent **0.45**. Preview the complete reset/preserve consequences, then confirm. The full candidate ledger is saved before adoption; failed writes preserve the run, and stale/repeated/unissued claims cannot reuse its earnings.
- Prestige resets normal money, upgrades, cultivation/discovery, knives and modifications. Seeds, permanent investments/discoveries, lifetime statistics and preferences survive.
- **Permanent Slice Value** costs 1 seed initially and immediately adds 25% cut value per level. It reveals **Starting Cash**, initially 2 seeds, granting $5 per level on future prestige resets. Each has five levels and doubles its price per level.
- Permanent discoveries use the existing botanical vines, quiet fades, clipped-title acknowledgement, unread dots and Reduced Motion. An original envelope/seal and restrained prestige cue are included; the camera stays fixed.
- Validation: **477 headless**, **130 rendered GUI**, and **48 embedded-pack checks per export target** pass; complete source/import and package validation are recorded in the handoff.
- Save schema **5** migrates v1–v4 normal progress, including existing lifetime earnings in the first cycle, without granting free seeds/upgrades. New Game separately confirms clearing permanent progress and retains preferences. All existing varieties/equipment/abilities remain integrated.

Phase 5 is in progress: broader permanent branches and five dedicated automatic events remain. This is a development milestone with three of 25 normal varieties, three of 25 knives, eighteen normal upgrades, one preparation and two permanent investments. The next task is the separately gated Golden Harvest event. The model reaches eligibility at 74.0 minutes under its 80%-accuracy assumption, 45.5 minutes at perfect accuracy, and not within 90 minutes at 55%; this is not human balance validation. Native Windows, Forward+ GPU, audible quality and target-PC 60 FPS remain unverified. See Source/PROGRESS.md and Source/TESTING.md for exact evidence.
